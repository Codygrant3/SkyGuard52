## Best closure route

### 1. Commission or capture the identity-defining recordings

Prioritize project-owned recording agreements for:

- Yak-52 rear-cockpit idle, cruise, high-load and transition passes;
- exterior radial engine and propeller perspectives;
- open rear-canopy airflow at representative safe flight states;
- Ukrainian and English pilot/controller/radio performances.

Obtain signed location, aircraft/operator, performer and recording releases.
Do not conduct weapons or missile recording without appropriately licensed
professional facilities and legal supervision.

### 2. License specialist libraries for inaccessible military effects

Use a reputable commercial sound-effects publisher for:

- rifle muzzle, action, casing and distant tails matched to the final weapon;
- shoulder-launched missile search, lock, launch, fly-by and impact layers;
- piston-powered one-way UAV and heavier campaign drone signatures;
- close, medium and distant explosion layers.

The license must expressly allow modification, commercial interactive-game use,
embedding in packaged builds, and distribution to end users. Raw-library
redistribution must remain prohibited in project policy even when a license is
permissive.

### 3. Audition CC0 candidates before downloading

For every ledger candidate:

1. Listen on the source page.
2. Confirm semantic identity, perspective and useful duration.
3. Reject clipping, destructive denoising, obvious synthesis where authenticity
   is required, excessive ambience and non-loopable engine beds.
4. Confirm creator, page license and any described upstream source.
5. Only then authorize a download.

The radio candidate remains blocked even though its page displays CC0 because
underlying speech provenance and performer/privacy rights are not confirmed.

### 4. Preserve immutable acquisition evidence

On authorized download:

- place the original in a non-shipping provenance archive;
- calculate SHA-256 immediately;
- capture the source page as PDF or HTML with access date;
- record original filename, format, duration, sample rate, channels and bit depth;
- record creator, license, license URL, attribution and upstream-source notes;
- never overwrite the original.

Every edit receives a separate derivative filename, process description and
hash. Only derivatives enter Unreal.

### 5. Produce and integrate

- Standardize delivery to the sound designer’s approved sample rate/bit depth.
- Create clean loops and RPM/load transitions without phase discontinuities.
- Build separate muzzle/crack/body/debris/tail layers rather than one repeated
  “explosion” file.
- Author MetaSounds/Sound Cues, attenuation, concurrency and the seven required
  routing assets.
- Populate all 25 production-bank entries.
- Keep courtesy attribution for CC0 sources.

### 6. Pass audible acceptance

Run the packaged Development acceptance harness on an audible device with
calibrated metering. Capture:

- immutable build and evidence hashes;
- at least 600 measured samples;
- active voices and underruns;
- audio-thread cost;
- true peak;
- cockpit/exterior translation;
- rifle, Igla, drone, explosion and radio intelligibility;
- a combat soak with ADS, repeated shots, multiple drones and boss destruction.

Final acceptance remains blocked until every source and routing entry is
production-ready. The native procedural audition bank remains QA-only and
Shipping-disabled.
