# Yak-52 R6 Photo Intake Cycle03 Visual Review

Classification: `PASSED_PHOTOGRAPHIC_REFERENCE_INTAKE_ONLY`

All nine derived 1280×720 frames and the raw 1600×1079 exterior photograph
were opened and inspected directly at original resolution.

This review accepts the files as bounded internal photographic references. It
does not classify Yak-52 R6 as ready for Blender, Unreal import, or production
acceptance.

## Exterior side photograph

Useful evidence:

- full side silhouette and ground stance;
- cowling opening, propeller blur and spinner/hub appearance;
- canopy-bow count and approximate spacing;
- front and rear cockpit relationship;
- landing-gear layout;
- wing-root fairing, fuselage taper, horizontal tail and fin appearance;
- panel, fastener and paint-weathering language.

Limits:

- perspective photograph, not an orthographic drawing;
- landing attitude and lens distortion prevent authoritative dimension
  extraction;
- rear canopy is closed;
- visible Airliners.net and Kelvin Jahae marks require reference-only handling.

## Production implications

The R6 asset and Gate 7 character/weapon contracts should use this package to
validate:

- rear-gunner camera height and side-firing composition;
- open rear-canopy rim and bow visibility;
- rifle and hand placement without canopy, wing or pilot clipping;
- wing-skin fastener density, access panels and control-surface seams;
- exterior paint roughness, wear and grime scale;
- transient shadows of canopy, gunner and weapon on the wing.

The imagery must not be used to:

- infer exact dimensions;
- copy copyrighted pixels into textures, decals, UI or marketing;
- reproduce identifiable people as character likenesses;
- replace the required technical-reference set.

## Remaining R6 blockers

1. Dimensioned side, top and front three-view or equivalent manual drawings.
2. Fuselage stations or maintenance cross-sections.
3. Canopy rails, bow locations, overlap and rear-canopy travel.
4. Cowling, M-14P engine face, V-530 propeller plane and installation geometry.
5. Wing planform, root chord/fairing, incidence and dihedral.
6. Tailplane and fin planform/dimensions.
7. The three earlier interior/cockpit temporary images are no longer available
   at their original paths and would need to be attached again for governed
   intake.

Blender and Unreal remain unauthorized for Yak-52 R6 production.
