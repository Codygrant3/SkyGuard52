# Phase 5 P5-A — Network Source Research

Research on 2026-08-02 found a credible exact-aircraft commercial library:
Pro Sound Effects exposes 87 Yak-52 source pages across its Foundation and
Aviation collections. The selected pages identify a 1977 or 1979 Yak-52,
creator Stephan Schutze, stereo WAV delivery, perspective/state descriptions
and duration.

The publisher's licensing pages describe a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free
commercial synchronization license for a single user, permit editing and
looping, and prohibit standalone redistribution. Multi-user access requires a
separate license. No purchase, preview download or audio download occurred.

## Conditional production candidates

- Engine idle: 1979 Yak-52 interior idle, 55 seconds.
- Engine cruise: two 1979 Yak-52 flying-interior alternatives, 77 and 86
  seconds.
- Engine power: 1979 Yak-52 takeoff interior, 47 seconds.
- Propeller: 1977 Yak-52 exterior idle explicitly described with propeller
  whir, 54 seconds.

These are candidates for licensed audition—not accepted production sources.
RPM/load, exact rear-cockpit microphone position, loopability, contamination,
technical format and the final purchased license evidence remain unresolved.

## Reference-only

High-idle and system-check recordings can inform identity but do not match
stable power/idle beds. Wikimedia Commons has an eleven-second exact Yak-52
startup video under CC BY-SA 4.0; its duration, exterior perspective,
video-derived audio and share-alike obligations keep it reference-only.

## Blocked

No source page explicitly proves open-rear-canopy Yak-52 airflow at documented
airspeed. Interior flight recordings cannot silently substitute for it.

Eagle Dynamics states that DCS: Yak-52 uses sounds recorded from a Yak-52, but
its EULA reserves those sounds and prohibits reverse engineering. Installed DCS
audio is therefore blocked from reuse or extraction.

Before any purchase or download, request written confirmation from

cooked Windows-game distribution, editing/looping/layering, promotional use,

recording metadata. Also ask whether the catalog contains an explicit
