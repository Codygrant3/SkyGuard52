# BLD-M01-COAST-PROD-001 Visual Review

Status: `ACCEPTED_AS_LAYOUT_SCAFFOLD_REJECTED_AS_VISIBLE_AAA_ART`

The source and artifact gates pass. The package is useful as a governed
dimension, snapping, collision and composition scaffold, but it is not
acceptable as the visible final-quality coastal environment.

## Accepted uses

- Retain the four exact-dimension 100 m by 80 m terrain districts as hidden
  layout underlayers and snap references.
- Retain the continuous beach, dune, seawall, road and sidewalk transitions as
  route-composition guides.
- Retain sockets, pivots, UV channels, collision intent and governed asset
  naming as the production integration contract.
- Retain street and drainage modules for scale reference and later
  component-by-component replacement.

## Blocking findings

1. Terrain surfaces are visibly flat and use diagnostic checker materials
   rather than calibrated sand, soil, dune vegetation, concrete, asphalt and
   wetness layers.
2. The midrise family reads as plain box shells without reference-faithful
   facade depth, window construction, balconies, entrances, roof equipment,
   utility routing or believable ground-floor frontage.
3. Street, drainage, curb and seawall modules lack close-range bevel,
   fastener, joint, damage, grime, salt, leak and debris detail.
4. Repetition is immediately visible across the building silhouettes and
   facade rhythm.
5. There are no convincing material transitions, decals, vertex-painted
   weathering, foliage integration or destruction variants.
6. The package does not meet the planned daylight, overcast, night, wet and
   storm visual-validation standard.

## Production direction

Do not use the exported midrise shells or diagnostic terrain surfaces as
player-visible AAA art.

Use this package as the stable spatial skeleton beneath a quarantined
Fab/Quixel visible-art pass. Inspect one Eastern European city kit and one
beach/coast kit first, record license and technical provenance, and replace
visible surfaces in bounded modules while preserving the accepted dimensions,
route clearance, collision and district snapping contract.

The next visible-art gate must require:

- reference-faithful Ukrainian coastal facade language;
- calibrated PBR sand, asphalt, concrete, plaster, metal and glass;
- Nanite/LOD, collision and texture-memory evidence;
- close, route-distance and aerial comparison renders;
- at least three non-repeating facade and terrain compositions;
- daylight, overcast, night, wet and storm checks;
- no change to the exact 100 m district snapping or protected flight corridor;
- matched performance evidence before runtime promotion.
