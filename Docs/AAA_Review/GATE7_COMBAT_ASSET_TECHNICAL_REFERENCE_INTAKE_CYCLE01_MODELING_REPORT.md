## Outcome

`BLOCKED_AWAITING_REFERENCE_INPUT`

The search materially improves modeling authority, but it does not yet authorize a final Blender production pass. It authorizes bounded blockouts for the 9K38 missile, human limb proportions, a Shahed-136 exterior envelope, and a MIL-STD-1913 rail test coupon.

## Strongest measured findings

- 9K38 missile: 1,574 mm long and 72 mm diameter.
- NASA human envelope: hand length 158–221 mm, hand breadth 71–102 mm, shoulder-to-elbow 296–419 mm, and forearm-plus-hand 387–546 mm.
- Shahed-136 official-handbook blockout: 3,300 mm long and 3,000 mm span, pending resolution of conflicting reports and missing measured cross-sections.
- MIL-STD-1913: the rail and recoil-groove figures are sufficient for a standards-constrained test coupon.

## Rifle finding

The footage and current proxy are consistent with an AR/M4-pattern configuration, but they do not prove an exact M4A1 configuration. The Colt M4A1 sheet is therefore a candidate comparison, while the AK-74 sheet is a second comparison. Neither is accepted as the in-game rifle until footage frames or another primary record freeze the exact receiver, barrel, handguard, stock, sights, magazine, and finish.

## Igla finding

The 9K38 global missile dimensions are usable for blockout. Public visual records mix 9K38, Igla-S/9K338, and training/visual-simulator hardware, so launcher details remain blocked. The model must not blend their nose, battery, gripstock, sight, or rear-cap features.
