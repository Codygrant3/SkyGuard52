# M01 Fab Visible-Art Shortlist

Status: `CATALOG_RESEARCH_ONLY`

Research date: 2026-08-02

Nothing in this list has been purchased, downloaded, licensed to the project,
or imported. Prices and availability are catalog observations and can change.
Every selected asset still requires product-page review, license capture,
technical inspection, a quarantined Unreal import and performance testing.

## Highest-value city candidates

1. [Soviet Panels - Communist Eastern European Buildings Kit](https://www.fab.com/listings/e0fb40c4-05a7-4e49-9fd6-a10bbcca0ef8)
   by PolyGo Studios — observed from $89.99.
   Best thematic match for the M01 Ukrainian coastal midrise layer. Audit
   modularity, interior visibility, Nanite support, material instances,
   collision and draw calls before acquisition.
2. [Modular European Buildings (Facades)](https://www.fab.com/listings/cf0ba648-c4cf-4075-93fb-72fe98321c46)
   by Cattus SP — observed from $39.99, 4.1/5 from nine catalog ratings.
   Potential facade layer over the governed coastal shell/snap kit.
3. [European Alleys](https://www.fab.com/listings/7587af2d-3c9c-46c7-b0f6-a87fcb3ff180)
   by Anil Isbilir — observed from $69.99, 4.7/5 from fifteen catalog ratings.
   Candidate for close-route streets, props and breakup of repeated facades.
4. [Eastern European Trees](https://www.fab.com/listings/6ce041ff-9fc6-466a-a574-33e5e681dc34)
   by Alex Vikodin — observed from $12.99, 5.0/5 from one catalog rating.
   Candidate regional vegetation set, subject to species, wind, seasonal,
   collision and foliage-density review.

## Highest-value coast candidates

1. [Brushify - Beach Pack](https://www.fab.com/listings/72a92a2e-3c52-46a5-9f17-6cf1f5d35006)
   by JoeGarth — observed from $49.99, 4.3/5 from sixteen catalog ratings.
   Strong candidate for visible landscape material, shoreline blending and
   reusable beach dressing over the 100 m governed terrain scaffold.
2. [Beach Cliffs](https://www.fab.com/listings/de03932e-d4ad-4c9b-9360-b2ab1634ab07)
   by Bearly3D — observed from $28.99, 5.0/5 from three catalog ratings.
   Candidate for route landmarks and breaking the flat slab silhouette.
3. [Beach and Coast Environment](https://www.fab.com/listings/47843f8c-aaa9-43fd-9b69-207cd1172389)
   by StudioD3D — observed from $79.99, 5.0/5 from one catalog rating.
   Broad candidate pack, but must be checked for overlap and content bloat
   before preferring it to narrower packs.
4. [Coastal Sea Rocks Vol. 1](https://www.fab.com/listings/c621214f-e296-4485-85c3-100604a2444e)
   by Unimodels — observed from $4.99.
   Low-cost candidate for shoreline repetition breakup after texture and
   Nanite review.

## Acquisition rule

Do not buy the list wholesale. First inspect the Soviet Panels, Modular
European Facades and Brushify Beach Pack product pages in the signed-in Fab
session. Record:

- exact Fab license and acquisition receipt;
- supported Unreal versions and platform restrictions;
- source size and expected installed size;
- mesh/material/texture counts and maximum texture resolution;
- Nanite, Lumen, collision, LOD/HLOD and PCG support;
- whether assets are modular at Skyguard's 1 m/10 cm snap standards;
- whether redistribution inside a cooked Windows game is covered;
- whether the visible result can pass the M01 route and frame-time budgets.

Only one city kit and one beach/surface kit should enter the first quarantine
import. The Blender coast package remains the dimension, snap, collision and
seam scaffold; Fab assets supply the visible surface and authored detail.
