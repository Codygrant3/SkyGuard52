## Direct image inspection

### Pathfinder

The three-quarter and grazing views are severely overexposed. Large surfaces resolve as featureless white, preventing evaluation of material response, topology, bevel quality, wear, or readable silhouette transitions. The asset is visibly assembled from disconnected slabs and primitives, with two detached cylinders and multiple floating members. Framing leaves excessive empty space and clips required bounds. This is proxy geometry, not a production hero asset.

### Lighthouse

The views show a stack of simple cylindrical forms with nearly featureless white shading. The base is cropped, the lantern room and gallery are not production-readable, and there is no convincing masonry, glazing, railing, roof, door, service detail, or weathering. Exposure prevents PBR evaluation.

### Radar post

The views show a thin pole-like proxy with sparse cross-members. The base is cropped and the upper element reads as detached. There is no production radar assembly, platform, access hardware, cabling, material separation, or weathering. Exposure again prevents PBR evaluation.
