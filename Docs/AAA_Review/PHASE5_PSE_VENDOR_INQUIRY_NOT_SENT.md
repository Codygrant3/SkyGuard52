# NOT SENT — Pro Sound Effects Licensing and Metadata Inquiry

**Status:** DRAFT ONLY — NOT SENT  
**Intended recipient:** licensing@prosoundeffects.com  
**Project:** Skyguard 52, a commercial Windows game built in Unreal Engine  
**No purchase, download, license grant or vendor contact is implied by this draft.**

## Proposed subject

License and recording-metadata questions for Yak-52 sounds in a commercial
Unreal Engine game

## Draft message

Hello Pro Sound Effects licensing team,

I am evaluating several exact-aircraft Yak-52 recordings in your Foundation
and Aviation catalogs for a commercial Windows game:

- Aircraft Yakovlev Yak52 1979 Idle Interior01 Stereo (`PSE_SL-FL/D6AlU`)
- Aircraft Yakovlev Yak52 1979 Flying Interior01 Stereo (`PSE_SL-FL/pcON0`)
- Aircraft Yakovlev Yak52 1979 Flying Interior02 Stereo (`PSE_SL-FL/TR4z6`)
- Aircraft Yakovlev Yak52 1979 Take Off Interior01 Stereo (`PSE_SL-FL/NbnZ4`)
- Aircraft 1977 Yakovlev Yak52 Idle01 Stereo (`PSE_SL-AC/Wq32C`)

Before purchasing or downloading anything, could you please confirm in writing:

1. Which license is appropriate for our actual number of people who may access
   the raw files during development? We understand the standard lifetime
   license is for one user and that multiple users require different coverage.
2. Does that license permit:
   - commercial Windows-game synchronization;
   - distribution inside cooked Unreal Engine IoStore containers;
   - editing, trimming, equalization, layering, looping and pitch/RPM
     processing;
   - use of the synchronized results in trailers, store videos and other
     promotion?
3. What safeguards do you require to prevent customers from obtaining the
   sounds as standalone files? We intend to exclude originals and working WAVs
   from the packaged build and distribute only cooked game assets.
4. Are these sounds available individually, or does proper licensing require a
   Foundation, Aviation or other library purchase?
5. Can you provide recorder notes for the selected files, including:
   - aircraft year/configuration and, where known, registration;
   - engine and propeller configuration;
   - RPM/load or operating state;
   - microphone model, placement and interior seat position;
   - canopy position;
   - original sample rate, bit depth and channel layout?
6. Do you have any Yak-52 recording that explicitly captures rear-cockpit
   airflow with the rear canopy open at a documented airspeed and canopy
   opening? We have not treated generic interior flight or synthetic wind as a
   substitute.

If the selected license is suitable, please provide a quote or checkout path
that identifies the licensed products, license type, covered users/entity,
term, territory and permitted media. We will preserve the invoice and governing
license as project provenance.

Thank you.
